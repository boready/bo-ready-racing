Sponsorship Guide

How Race Car Sponsorship Works for Kansas Businesses

Supporting a local dirt track team can put a business in front of racing fans while helping a driver compete throughout the season. Ready Racing #38 offers partnership opportunities built around a Kansas non-wing micro sprint race car.

What can a sponsor receive?

Depending on the agreement, a partner may receive logo placement on the car, team Facebook mentions, a profile on the team website, sponsor features in race-related content, and opportunities to collaborate on promotions or appearances.

How is placement decided?

Car logo size, location, content frequency, and any exclusivity should be agreed upon before a sponsorship begins. The team and sponsor should also confirm which assets and approvals are needed.

Does sponsorship guarantee sales?

No. Sponsorship provides promotional opportunities, not guaranteed impressions, customers, or revenue. Reporting can include delivered posts, links, and available campaign metrics.
