PRIVACY

Short version: we collect only what we need to answer sponsor inquiries and to show partners that real fans show up. We don't sell data, and we don't use advertising trackers or cookies.

Sponsor inquiries

When you send the inquiry form we store your business name, name, email, optional phone, package interest, your message, and (if you used the location match) the name of your market. We use it only to reply to you about sponsorship. Ask us to delete it any time through the team's official social channels.

Location match and track check-in

This is entirely optional and only runs when you press a button and approve your browser's location prompt. Your coordinates are sent once to our server, compared with a list of zones (Kansas, Tulsa, race venues), and then discarded. We never store coordinates. We keep only the matched zone name, and for check-ins an anonymous one-way hash so one fan counts once per venue per day. Entering a ZIP code instead only uses its first three digits.

Anonymous site statistics

We count page sections viewed and button clicks using a random ID kept in your browser's local storage (not a cookie, not linked to your identity, and hashed again on our server). No IP addresses are stored with these statistics. Clear your browser's site data to reset the ID.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them. Inquiry alerts may be forwarded to a private team notification channel.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reach the team on the official Bo Ready Racing social channels. Back to the site
