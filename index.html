01 / ABOUT BO READYTHE STORY BEHIND #38

U.S. ARMY VETERAN / FOUNDER OF BO READY RACING

TEN YEARS
OF SERVICE.
A LIFETIME
OF DREAMING.

ONE DECISION CHANGED EVERYTHING.

I've always dreamed about racing. It was something I wanted to do for as long as I can remember, but like a lot of things in life, there was always a reason to put it off.

I spent 10 years serving in the United States Army. During that time, I learned about discipline, hard work, determination, and what it means to be part of a team. Those lessons stayed with me long after my military service ended.

But the dream of racing never went away.

Then one day, I woke up and decided I was done talking about it. I was going to buy a race car. And that's exactly what I did.

I didn't get into racing because I had everything figured out. I got into it because it was something I'd always wanted to do, and I finally decided to take the chance. That's how Bo Ready Racing #38 got started.

Since then, I've learned that racing is about a whole lot more than getting behind the wheel. It's the late nights working on the car, figuring things out as you go, loading up for the track, and putting in the time and money to do something you love.

Some nights go your way. Some nights don't. But every time we roll into the pits, we're there because we want to be.

I'm proud to be a veteran, proud of what we're building, and thankful for the family, friends, and sponsors who help make it possible.

I spent years dreaming about racing.
Now I'm living it.
